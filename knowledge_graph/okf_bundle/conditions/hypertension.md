# Definition
- WHO: "Hypertension (high blood pressure) is when the pressure in your blood vessels is too high (140/90 mmHg or higher)."[^who]
- CDC: "High blood pressure is consistently at or above 130/80 mm Hg."[^cdc]
- The two thresholds differ by source; see [US 130/80](/criteria/bp-thresholds-130-80.md) and [ESC/ESH 140/90](/criteria/bp-thresholds-140-90.md).

# Consequences
- CDC: "High blood pressure increases the risk for heart disease and stroke, two leading causes of death for Americans."[^cdc]
