# Definition
- WHO: "Type 2 diabetes affects how your body uses sugar (glucose) for energy. It stops the body from using insulin properly, which can lead to high levels of blood sugar if not treated."[^who]
- CDC: "With type 2 diabetes, your body doesn't use insulin well and can't keep blood sugar at normal levels."[^cdc]

# Complications
- WHO: people with diabetes have "a higher risk of health problems including heart attack, stroke and kidney failure."[^who]
